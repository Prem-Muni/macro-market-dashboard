PRACTICAL REFERENCE · 16 SCORING RULES

Know exactly what earns a health point.

The current model uses six shared macro signals and ten signals specific to each ETF. One point is awarded for a valid state that is not classified bearish, including neutral states. The individual rules below tell you what that means.

Higher score = healthier conditions; this is not a count of purely bullish indicators or a forecast of returns.

01 / BOTH MARKETS · 6 SCORED SIGNALS

Macro conditions

Each shared macro signal contributes to both SPY and QQQ. Red/bearish states earn zero health points; other valid scored states earn one.

6 signals
01

Crude Oil

Oil is a cyclical inflation / growth proxy; the dashboard tracks its daily direction rather than its absolute level.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

Daily oil price change is positive (oil rises).

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Oil flat → CAUTION; falling → BULLISH. The “DIV” suffix means oil and SPY moved in the same direction that day; it is a label, not another point.

02

US Dollar

A proxy for changes in dollar strength. The model treats a stronger dollar as a risk headwind.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

Daily USD index/proxy change is positive.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Flat → CAUTION; falling → BULLISH. “DIV” is a same-direction comparison with SPY, not a second penalty.

03

10Y Yield

Daily direction of the 10-year Treasury yield proxy, often associated with discount-rate pressure.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

Daily 10-year yield change is positive.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Flat → CAUTION; falling → BULLISH. The “DIV” annotation compares its direction with SPY.

04

Credit · HYG / IEF

HYG represents high-yield corporate bonds; IEF represents intermediate Treasury bonds. Relative daily performance is a simple credit-risk appetite check.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

HYG one-day percentage return is lower than IEF one-day percentage return.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

HYG return equal to or above IEF → BULLISH. This checks daily returns, not the raw price ratio against a moving average.

05

Copper

A cyclical industrial-metal proxy; weakening copper can signal softer growth expectations.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

Daily copper price change is negative.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Unchanged or rising copper → BULLISH.

06

VIX Curve

Compares short-dated VIX with three-month implied volatility (VIX3M). An inverted / backwardated curve can signal near-term stress.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

VIX ÷ VIX3M is greater than 1.00.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Below 0.90 → BULLISH (contango). 0.90 through 1.00 → CAUTION / shallow contango / flat; 1.00 is not bearish.

S&P 500 indicators

Ten SPY-specific signals. Read the bearish condition for zero points; every other valid scored state earns one health point.

10 signals
01

VIX

S&P 500 option-implied volatility; a short-term measure of expected market movement.

VIX greater than 20.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Below 16 → BULLISH; 16–20 inclusive → CAUTION.

02

Defensives / SPY

An equal-weighted basket of XLP (consumer staples), XLV (healthcare), and XLU (utilities), compared with SPY.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

Average 10-session return of XLP, XLV and XLU exceeds SPY’s 10-session return.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

If the basket leads on 1D but not 10D → CAUTION; otherwise BULLISH. Measures sector rotation, not certainty of a market decline.

03

XLE / SPY

Energy sector ETF (XLE) 10-session return compared with the S&P 500 ETF (SPY).

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

XLE 10-session percentage return exceeds SPY’s.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Equal or lower energy-sector return → BULLISH. Energy outperformance is interpreted as defensive/inflation-related rotation in this experimental model.

04

SMH / SPY

Semiconductor ETF (SMH) trend and its relative leadership versus SPY.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

SMH closes below its own 20-day EMA.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

If SMH is at/above its EMA but SMH/SPY relative ratio is below its 15D SMA → LAGGING / CAUTION; otherwise BULLISH. “BEARISH DIV” notes SMH weakness while SPY is at/above its 20D EMA.

05

SPY Trend

The original scored trend rule based on SPY’s close, 50-day simple moving average and 200-day simple moving average.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

SPY close is below its 200-day SMA.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

BULLISH only if SPY close > 50D SMA and 50D SMA > 200D SMA. All other combinations → CAUTION. This remains the scored trend signal.

06

SPY RSI (14)

Wilder-smoothed 14-session Relative Strength Index, a momentum oscillator from 0 to 100.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

RSI(14) is below 45.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

RSI 45–55 inclusive → CAUTION; above 55 → BULLISH. Separate chart labels of overbought (≥70) or oversold (≤30) are descriptive, not extra score points.

07

SPY MACD (12, 26, 9)

Daily MACD line compared with its nine-session EMA signal line. Calculated using completed ETF daily closes.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

MACD line below the signal line (negative histogram).

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

MACD line at or above the signal line (histogram ≥ 0). This is a momentum check, not a standalone trade recommendation.

08

RSP / SPY

Equal-weighted S&P 500 (RSP) relative to cap-weighted SPY. Tests whether gains are broad or concentrated.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

RSP/SPY price ratio is more than 0.25% below its own 10D SMA.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

More than 0.25% above its 10D SMA → BULLISH; within ±0.25% inclusive → CAUTION.

09

IWM / SPY

Russell 2000 small-cap ETF (IWM) relative to SPY. Gauges small-cap participation.

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

IWM/SPY price ratio is more than 0.25% below its own 10D SMA.

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

More than 0.25% above → BULLISH; ±0.25% inclusive → CAUTION.

10

S&P Breadth · 1D

Daily change in the percentage of S&P 500 constituents trading above their own 200-day SMAs. Expressed in percentage points (pp).

0 or 1 health pt
BEARISH STATE · 0 HEALTH PTS

Breadth falls by at least 1 percentage point in one session (change ≤ −1 pp).

OTHER STATES · +1 HEALTH PT

Change ≥ +1 pp → EXPANDING / BULLISH; between −1 and +1 pp → STABLE / CAUTION. Website breadth may use estimated constituent-derived data.

04 / REFERENCE ONLY

Data displayed but not scored

These are supporting readings. They do not add extra points beyond the 16 scored signals.

0 points

SPY / QQQ · 5, 20, 50, 100 and 200D SMAs

The simple moving average is the mean of the last N completed ETF closing prices. Each card displays its average and the percentage difference from the latest close.

BULLISH: latest close ≥ SMA (including equality). BEARISH: latest close < SMA. These individual comparisons add no points; the separate original Trend signal remains scored.

S&P Breadth · 5D

Five-session change in the percentage of S&P 500 constituents above their own 200D moving averages. ≤ −2 percentage points = CONTRACTING; ≥ +2 pp = EXPANDING; otherwise STABLE. Not in the score.

S&P Breadth · 200D level

Current percentage of S&P 500 constituents above their own 200D averages. Below 40% = BEARISH; 40% to less than 60% = CAUTION; ≥60% = BULLISH. Not in the score.

Nasdaq Breadth · 5D

Difference between the latest five-session average advancing share and the preceding five-session average, in percentage points. ≤ −5 pp = CONTRACTING; ≥ +5 pp = EXPANDING; otherwise STABLE. Not in the score.

RSI chart band vs. RSI score

RSI is scored once below 45, even though its chart also displays classic labels: ≤30 = OVERSOLD, ≥70 = OVERBOUGHT, and everything between = NEUTRAL. Those chart bands are not additional points.

SPY / QQQ charts & Fear & Greed

TradingView's hosted ETF price charts update independently of the EOD score engine. The separate FearGreedChart.com sentiment series is not the CNN index. Neither chart contributes Market Health points. The website has no put/call ratio component.
