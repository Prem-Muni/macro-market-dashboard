WHY THE THRESHOLDS DIFFER

Same 15 indicators. Two Risk-On cutoffs.

This page records what the model does, why the threshold was chosen, and what the historical evidence actually showed. It is intended as a durable reference for future reviews and anyone with whom the dashboard is shared.

Each scored bearish condition contributes one point. A lower score means fewer selected risk conditions. Risk-On is a market-regime label, not a prediction, a guarantee, or a direct buy instruction.

DECISION RATIONALE

Why we made this distinction

1 · Both use the same scoring framework

Six shared macro indicators plus nine index-specific indicators. We do not add or remove indicators for QQQ; only the Risk-On classification boundary differs.

2 · A score of 5 is not “strong bullish”

In QQQ it is classified Risk-On for usability, but explicitly labeled borderline cautious. We still consider five-day trend, breadth, volatility, and market confirmation before making decisions.

3 · This is a judgment call, not proof

Reconstructed 20-month results do not show that QQQ 0–5 improves the 0–4 baseline. We accept the extra uncertainty as a chosen, more permissive growth-market threshold and will revisit it as new data accumulates.

QQQ tracks roughly 100 large non-financial Nasdaq-listed companies and is technology- and growth-heavy, not exclusively technology stocks. Its higher historical volatility is context for the policy choice, but volatility by itself is not evidence that adding score 5 improves subsequent returns.

How to interpret the decision responsibly

Important: This is a discretionary classification based on preliminary, reconstructed daily-bar research—not proof that QQQ score 5 provides an advantage. Five-session return windows overlap; the sample is short; historical breadth is estimated using today's constituents (survivorship bias); prices omit dividends, slippage, and costs. Selecting a cutoff after looking at the data also introduces selection bias. Risk-On does not mean risk-free, and the separate Swing Environment label may disagree because it applies additional conditions. Reassess with more periods and genuine out-of-sample data.
