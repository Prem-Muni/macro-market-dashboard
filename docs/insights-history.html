How the test works · limitations and bias

Uses the same end-of-day price and reconstructed indicator inputs as the current 16-point dashboard. It evaluates the following completed sessions, so the signal itself uses no future prices. Full 16/16 signal coverage is required. The last 10 sessions may have no mature 10-session outcome.

The sample uses reconstructed historical breadth based partly on today's constituents and can have survivorship bias. Return windows overlap. The non-overlapping 5-session sensitivity check offers fewer, less-dependent observations but is not a strategy simulation. Outcomes omit dividends, transaction costs, and slippage. Thresholds were chosen after earlier research, so this cannot establish statistical significance or independent predictive skill.

How to use this archive

Each dated row captures the selected ETF's completed close, day-over-day ETF price change when available, daily Market Health Score, daily change in Market Health points, risk regime, 5-day trend, data coverage and matching price/risk alert. Select Signals to inspect the same-session changes available in the engine's snapshot.

Scores use the website's experimental 16-point model; older archived sessions retain their original 15-point scale. Thinkorswim is calculated independently using its corresponding 16-point study; price sources and timing may differ. Constituent-derived breadth may be estimated, and observations are not proof of predictive accuracy. Archive persistence: data is stored in docs/data/latest.json and included in the existing GitHub Actions snapshot commit. November 20 is a user-screenshot backfill; its prior ETF close and price/risk alert are intentionally unavailable. Download a CSV periodically for a personal backup.
